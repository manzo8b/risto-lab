# Matrice Allergeni 2.7 — targeted semantic revision

Regional pasta forms remain composition hypotheses unless explicit ingredient evidence is available. Exact alternatives with a shared direct allergen group are recognised; optional, mixed-group and complex alternatives remain doubts. Dotted DOP/IGP/PDO abbreviations are normalised. Parma ham has a distinct identity and a specification check, not an absence declaration. Specific mozzarella, dry durum pasta, savoiardi, pure cocoa and animal-source gelatine lexical forms are introduced; generic stock/chocolate/gelatine remain conservative. Pine nuts are reported separately, outside EU Annex II.
